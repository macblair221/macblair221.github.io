Parkinson's medication-state classifier

EEG, MNE-BIDS, scikit-learn, permutation testing

Classifies whether a Parkinson's patient is on or off levodopa from resting EEG. The first pipeline reported 0.796 ROC-AUC, but its random split leaked patient identity. Rebuilt with leave-one-subject-out evaluation, it holds at 0.578 on unseen patients and beats a permutation null (p = 0.0099).

  • Extracted 48 features across 7 sensorimotor channels from raw OpenNeuro clinical recordings.
  • Removed two paths by which recording gain leaked into the label, using relative band power and a normalized coupling index.
  • Traced the two best-scoring subjects to a preprocessing mismatch flagged in the dataset's own metadata.
