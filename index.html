Teaching a robot arm by demonstration

LeRobot SO-101, ACT policy, PS4 teleoperation, Weights & Biases

I built a 6-DoF follower arm from assembly through deployment of a learned policy. It now picks up a block and places it in a bowl on its own.

  • Wrote a PS4 teleoperation script to record demonstrations
  • Recorded 50+ episodes and trained an Action Chunking Transformer on them
  • Deployed the policy back onto the arm and tested it on real hardware

Training loss fell from 0.35 to 0.06. The dataset (so101_pick_and_place_v3) and model (so101-block-place-final) are on Hugging Face.

Parkinson's medication state from EEG, and why the first answer was wrong

MNE-BIDS, scikit-learn, random forest, permutation testing

A classifier that tells whether a Parkinson's patient is on or off levodopa from resting EEG. The first pipeline scored 0.796, but its random split let the model recognize individual patients. Rebuilt with leave-one-subject-out testing, it holds at 0.578 on patients it has never seen, above a permutation null.

  • Processed raw clinical recordings from OpenNeuro into 48 features across 7 sensorimotor channels
  • Switched to relative band power and a normalized coupling index, closing two ways recording gain leaked into the label
  • Traced the two best-scoring subjects to a preprocessing mismatch flagged in the dataset's own metadata
  • Tested against 100 shuffled-label runs (0.500 ± 0.012, p = 0.0099)

An ECG front-end from off-the-shelf parts

AD620 instrumentation amp, Arduino, PySerial, MNE-Python, SciPy

A full pipeline that captures, filters, and analyzes a live heartbeat. I designed it with EEG as the end goal and validated it on ECG first, because the stronger signal makes it possible to check every stage.

  • Built an amplifier circuit that pulls a tiny biological signal out of electrical noise
  • Streamed it through an Arduino at about 190 Hz into Python
  • Removed baseline wander and interference with band-pass filtering
  • Wrote peak detection with a voltage floor to find each heartbeat and compute heart rate
